Hypotheses

There exist eta_i and G, obtained with the PINN, such that integrating the MPR model forward with them and passing the result through the Balloon-Windkessel model and the BOLD equation yields a functional connectivity (FC) that matches the real FC.
The data determine the parameters: repeated training with different seeds and different initial values of eta_i and G converges to the same values.
With synthetic data generated from known eta_i and G chosen away from the centre of the prior, the method recovers them.
